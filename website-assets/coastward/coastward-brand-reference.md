## One-line description

Coastward is a group trip planning app that replaces the spreadsheet-and-group-text chaos of organizing a trip with real people, with a shared space for deciding dates, destinations, lodging, and everything else — together.

## Short description (for a website product card)

> **Coastward** — Group trip planning, without the group text spiral. Coastward helps groups of any size agree on dates, vote on destinations, split lodging costs, and coordinate everything from meals to activities, all in one shared app.

## Longer description (for a dedicated product page)

> Planning a trip with a group usually means a scattered mess of texts, spreadsheets, and half-remembered decisions. Coastward brings it all into one place: propose trip dates and let everyone weigh in with a simple yes/maybe/no, vote on destination and lodging options with star ratings, split costs fairly, and keep track of activities, meals, and shopping lists as the trip comes together. It's built to feel simple enough for anyone in the group to use, while still handling the real complexity of planning a trip with other people.

## Logo / app icon (delivered assets)

**Composition:** a rust-colored circle ("sun") sitting above two horizontal wave strokes — a white wave, then a gold wave beneath it — evoking a coastal sunset/horizon. Set against the ink navy background for the main app icon.

**Files:**
- `icon.png` — full app icon, square, ink navy background, all three elements (sun + white wave + gold wave)

## Key features (for a bullet-point feature list)

- **Group decision-making** — vote on trip dates, rate destination and lodging options, settle disagreements with a Decision Wheel
- **Traveler roster** — track who's coming, grouped by household/family unit
- **Lodging comparison** — compare cost, photos, and amenities across multiple lodging options per destination
- **Fair expense splitting** — track and split costs per traveler or household group
- **Shared trip dashboard** — a single place for activities, meals, shopping lists, and trip photos once planning is locked in
- **Built for any group size** — designed around the reality that group trips involve real coordination, not just one person's itinerary
