Two products, two problems solved.

Both replace an improvisation that people are already stuck with — a spreadsheet and a group text, or a whiteboard on a shop floor — with a system built for that job specifically. Neither one is enterprise software scaled down.

Coastward

Group trip planning, without the group text spiral.

Coastward helps groups of any size agree on dates, vote on destinations, split lodging costs, and coordinate everything from meals to activities — in one shared space instead of a scattered mess of texts, spreadsheets, and half-remembered decisions.

  • Propose trip dates and settle them with simple yes / maybe / no voting
  • Compare lodging options on cost, photos, and amenities, then rate them
  • Split expenses fairly, per traveler or per household group
  • One shared dashboard for activities, meals, and shopping lists
In development · pre-launch iOS · Android

ChuckEd

The upgrade you can actually afford.

Job routing, scheduling, and tooling management built for small-to-mid machine shops — the affordable step up from spreadsheets, without the cost, complexity, or implementation timeline of a full ERP system.

  • See what is routed where, across the whole floor, at a glance
  • Catch at-risk jobs before they run late
  • Manage tooling alongside the schedule, not in a separate binder
  • Deliberately scoped: no invoicing or financials, hands off cleanly to your ERP
In development Built on a working shop floor
