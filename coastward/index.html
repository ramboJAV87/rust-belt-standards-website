Too many decisions, scattered across too many text threads.

Every group trip runs into the same problem. The decisions pile up, and they end up spread across a group chat, a shared spreadsheet, and whoever happens to remember what was agreed. Coastward puts all of it in one shared place, so the group can plan together instead of planning around each other.

Rust Belt Standards builds practical tools for real problems, and this one started with a familiar one: planning trips with family and friends. Coastward is built to do better than the group chat — simple enough for anyone in the group to use, without anyone having to be “the organized one.”

  • 01Who's actually coming?
  • 02What dates work for everyone?
  • 03Where are we staying?
  • 04Who's paying for what?

Coastward answers each of these in the app, where the whole group can see the answer.

Everything the trip needs, in one shared app.

From figuring out who's in, to deciding where to go, to the trip itself. Everyone in the group sees the same plan.

Getting the group to a plan

01

Roster

See who's confirmed, who's maybe, and who hasn't answered yet. Travelers are grouped by household, so families are counted the way they actually travel.

02

Trip dates & destinations

Propose options, everyone votes, and the group lands on an answer. Destinations and lodging work the same way, with ratings, so the favorite is obvious instead of argued over.
